# Entry 7
##### 6/5/26

<h1> Content</h1>
Before I did my in class presentation and I was watching other people do theirs I felt nervous. When I got called on I was kind of not expecting it so I felt a little shaky when I first got up in front of the class. Then I realized that I just had to talk about something that I had practice talking about and I am passionate about. What also helped me was thinking about how these people that I was preventing were all my friends and liked all of them and didn't feel threatened by them at all so I had nothing to be nervous about. I don't think I will ever get over that feeling you get when you know you will have to speak in front of people, but I do feel more in control. My biggest take away from the in class presentation is that although I might be scared to speak in front of other people as long as I know what I'm talking about and did all the work I was supposed to I will be fine. I really found the expo presentation a lot more stressful because I had to turn my 5 minute presentation into a 1 minute presentation. To do this I prioritized the things that I was more proud of and most comfortable talking about. I didn't just cut off 80% of the end of my presentation but I slowly grinded it down to one minute. I would time myself giving my presentation and in the mornings leading up to the presentation Wenjie and I would practice doing our presentation to each other. I think that these two things helped a lot because when it came to the Expo I felt prepared. The biggest take away from the Expo for me was to practice with other people.

<h1> Skills</h1>
One skill I learned was how to speak informally with people confidently even if you were nervous. This skill I think will help me in multiple aspects of life. I will have to do public speaking throughout my life so it's better to get good at it now and even if it's not in front of a group of people it still helps when speaking to others in a professional environment. Another skill that I developed was how to practice with other and reserved feedback. When I was practicing with Wenjie in the mornings, he gave me some feedback that I found very helpful. Not only did this feedback help me in my presentations but it will also help me in the future because I can carry this feedback and apply it to other situations and it has taught me to always be open to feedback.
